SARBANES-OXLEY (SOX) COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

This checklist provides a comprehensive guide to SOX compliance requirements for publicly traded companies.

SECTION 302: CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY FOR FINANCIAL REPORTS

CEO and CFO Certification Requirements:
□ Review all quarterly and annual reports
□ Certify reports do not contain material misstatements
□ Certify reports fairly present financial condition
□ Certify disclosure controls and procedures are effective
□ Sign certifications personally (no delegation)

Internal Controls Certification:
□ Design controls to ensure material information is disclosed
□ Evaluate effectiveness of controls within 90 days
□ Present conclusions about control effectiveness
□ Disclose significant control deficiencies to auditors
□ Disclose any fraud involving management

SECTION 404: MANAGEMENT ASSESSMENT OF INTERNAL CONTROLS

Control Environment:
□ Document tone at the top
□ Establish code of ethics
□ Define organizational structure
□ Assign authority and responsibility
□ Document HR policies and procedures

Risk Assessment:
□ Identify financial reporting risks
□ Assess fraud risk
□ Evaluate changes affecting controls
□ Document risk assessment process
□ Update assessments annually

Control Activities:
□ Document all key controls
□ Implement segregation of duties
□ Establish authorization procedures
□ Implement physical safeguards
□ Create reconciliation processes

Information and Communication:
□ Document information systems
□ Establish communication channels
□ Report control deficiencies
□ Maintain audit trails
□ Secure financial data

Monitoring:
□ Conduct ongoing monitoring
□ Perform separate evaluations
□ Remediate identified deficiencies
□ Report to audit committee
□ Document monitoring activities

SECTION 409: REAL-TIME DISCLOSURE

Disclosure Requirements:
□ Establish disclosure committee
□ Create disclosure evaluation process
□ Monitor for material events
□ File 8-K within 4 business days
□ Update prior disclosures as needed

SECTION 802: DOCUMENT RETENTION

Record Keeping Requirements:
□ Retain audit workpapers for 7 years
□ Preserve emails related to audits
□ Maintain financial records
□ Document retention policies
□ Implement litigation hold procedures

Prohibited Actions:
□ No destruction of audit documents
□ No alteration of records
□ No falsification of documents
□ Criminal penalties: up to 20 years

SECTION 806: WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTIONS

Required Protections:
□ Establish anonymous reporting mechanism
□ Prohibit retaliation
□ Document all complaints
□ Investigate promptly
□ Protect complainant identity

SECTION 906: CEO/CFO CERTIFICATION OF PERIODIC REPORTS

Criminal Certification:
□ Reports comply with SEC requirements
□ Information is fairly presented
□ Criminal penalties for false certifications
□ Up to $5 million fine / 20 years imprisonment

IT CONTROLS FOR SOX COMPLIANCE

Access Controls:
□ Implement role-based access
□ Enforce password policies
□ Review access rights quarterly
□ Remove access promptly on termination
□ Log all access attempts

Change Management:
□ Document change requests
□ Test changes before implementation
□ Obtain proper approvals
□ Maintain change logs
□ Separate development and production

Data Backup and Recovery:
□ Daily backup of financial data
□ Off-site storage
□ Regular recovery testing
□ Documented procedures
□ Maximum 24-hour recovery time

Security Controls:
□ Firewall protection
□ Intrusion detection
□ Encryption of sensitive data
□ Security monitoring
□ Incident response plan

ANNUAL COMPLIANCE ACTIVITIES

Q1 Activities:
□ Annual control risk assessment
□ Update control documentation
□ External auditor planning meetings

Q2 Activities:
□ Interim control testing
□ Address identified deficiencies
□ Management self-assessments

Q3 Activities:
□ Complete control testing
□ Prepare management assessment
□ External auditor interim review

Q4 Activities:
□ Final control evaluation
□ Management report preparation
□ Audit committee presentations
□ External auditor attestation

COMMON DEFICIENCIES TO AVOID

Material Weaknesses:
- Inadequate segregation of duties
- Lack of qualified accounting personnel
- Insufficient IT general controls
- Missing or inadequate policies
- Inadequate review procedures

Significant Deficiencies:
- Untimely account reconciliations
- Inadequate documentation
- Incomplete journal entry review
- Missing management review evidence
- Insufficient training

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Required Documents:
□ Control narratives
□ Process flowcharts
□ Risk control matrices
□ Test procedures
□ Test results
□ Deficiency evaluations
□ Remediation plans
□ Management certifications

PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE

Civil Penalties:
- SEC enforcement actions
- Fines up to $5 million (individuals)
- Fines up to $25 million (companies)
- Trading suspensions

Criminal Penalties:
- Section 802: Up to 20 years imprisonment
- Section 906: Up to 20 years imprisonment
- Section 1102: Up to 20 years imprisonment

This checklist should be reviewed and updated annually to ensure continued SOX compliance.
